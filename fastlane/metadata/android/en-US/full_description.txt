Kitae is a workout journal built around your training days. Plan your split once, with days like Push, Pull and Legs, then pick a day and tick off your sets as you go.

• Named training days instead of weekdays: train whichever day you're up for
• Sets planned ahead for every exercise, ready when you start
• Tick off each set, and keep unfinished ones as skipped or remove them from the plan
• A rest timer that starts when you finish a set and keeps counting with the screen off. Any exercise or set can have its own rest time
• How to do each exercise, and your progress on it over time
• A clock for the workout in progress, and the length of every past workout
• Warm-up, standard, failure, drop and rest-pause sets
• Kilograms or pounds
• Progress, history and backups, with all your data kept on your device

鍛え (kitae) is Japanese for forging and training: what lifting does to you, one session at a time.

Kitae is based on Kenko by LooKeR and is free software under the GPL-3.0 license.
